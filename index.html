01THE IDEA

Not just “someday.”
Start with a project.

Funngro is a platform where young people can discover projects created by companies and brands. A project might ask you to share feedback, make content, try an app or help spread the word.

Some projects offer rewards for eligible completed work. Each opportunity has its own brief, requirements and reward details, so check the information in the app before deciding whether it’s right for you.
